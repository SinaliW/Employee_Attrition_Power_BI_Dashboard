# Employee_Attrition_Power_BI_Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is a Power BI HR Analytics Dashboard built as part of a Statistics in Practice group assignment. It analyzes employee data to understand attrition patterns, engagement, and career growth factors within an organization.

## Objectives

* Analyze employee attrition and key drivers
* Understand job satisfaction and work-life balance
* Identify high-risk employees
* Support HR decision-making with data insights

## Key Insights

* Employees with less than 1 year at the company are more likely to leave.
* Most attrition happens in the first 1–3 years, showing weak early-career retention.
* Low monthly income is strongly linked to higher chances of leaving.
* Employees working overtime are more likely to quit, suggesting work–life balance issues.
* Higher attrition is seen in Sales, R\&D, and technical roles.
* Increasing salary reduces employee flight risk.
